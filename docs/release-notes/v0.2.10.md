## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.10 (pre-release)

Your previous visit to the track next to today, more of the app in Polish, and the right unit for a speed added from a session's VBO. It installs over 0.2.9 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Last time here

- When your library holds an earlier day on the same track, the day's **Overview** shows **Last time here** after **Progression**: the date and name of that visit, and its **Best lap** and **Theoretical best** next to today's, with the difference (today minus last time; green is faster today). The previous visit in the same car is used; if that car has no earlier visit to the track, the previous visit in any car, and the card names that car. A time shown as — means that day has none. A first visit, a track that was not recognised, a day with no ranked lap, or a day saved outside the library shows no card.

### Recordings

- A speed channel that a session's VBO adds to its RCZ recording now keeps the unit the VBO declares. Before, it was read in the unit chosen for unlabelled speeds. Values are never converted.

### Names and wording

- In Polish, the Diagnostics steps for combining VBO and RCZ, adding a session, and Coach are now translated.
- When a session's VBO and RCZ disagree on a channel, the message that they disagree names the channel as you named it in **Channel names**, a temperature in the app's language, and any other channel as recorded, so an oil pressure or gear channel is no longer called **Oil** or **Gearbox**.
- In English, the Corner Analyzer marker reads **Minimum speed**, and the segment editor switch reads **Move the neighbouring segment's boundary too**.
