## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.2 (pre-release)

Import as you drive, a Library that keeps every day by itself, a coach that looks at the whole day with one main focus, weather for each session, and the reviewed Polish translation. It installs over 0.2.1, 0.2.0, 0.1.0 and Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### Import sessions as you drive

- The first page is now **Import sessions**. Picked recordings join today's day, the same way a share from RaceChrono does, and the day opens straight away with Save ready.
- Going back from a day lists it on the first page with its sessions, best times and **Open the day**. The per-file review moved to the day's **⋮ › Add and review recordings…**.
- A new day does not open by itself while another day's unsaved changes wait to be restored.

### Every day kept in your Library

- Every day you import is **saved by itself** into the Library inside the app, on phones, Macs and PCs. No dialog is needed: a new day saves at once, and later changes save shortly after you stop editing or when you go back.
- **Library** on Import sessions lists your days by car, year, track and date, with sessions and best lap. You can rename cars and tracks there, or move a day to another car with **Change car**.
- On a Mac or PC, **⋮ › Export for Overlays…** writes a `.fetproject` copy for Overlays; the day stays in the Library. A `.fetproject` opened from elsewhere saves as before.
- Days saved on a phone or tablet before this version are not in the Library; open them with **Open a saved day…**.

### A better coach

- The coach judges patterns across the **whole day so far**: a mistake repeated at the same corner in earlier sessions counts, so short sessions can get a plan too.
- In sessions with three or more laps, **traffic, warm-up and cool-down laps** (more than 5% slower than the session median) are left out, and the coach lists which ones.
- The day's corners **stay the same** when sessions are added, so a tip points at the same corner all day.
- The Next session card leads with **one main focus**; the other tips are for once that feels settled, and an improvement to keep comes last.
- Each lap is compared with faster laps **within reach** (at most 1.5 s faster) first, rather than only the quickest laps of the day.
- Changes are **ranked by time lost** through the corner plus the straight right after it, shown as **Straight after it**. A corner with no straight after it is ranked on its own time.
- **Coasting** now means the throttle is off (under 3%); a stretch held at light throttle is not called coasting.
- A new tip, **Brake at the same point every lap**, appears when a session's braking points at a corner spread over at least 20 m, and 20 m more than across the day's three fastest laps.

### Weather for each session

- Each session with a recording time and GPS gets the weather at the track during the session (temperature, rain, wind, humidity, cloud, pressure), from Open-Meteo. It shows in Session details, Progression and under laps A and B in Compare, and is saved with the day so it reopens offline.
- Only the position rounded to about 1 km and the dates leave the device. Settings has a switch to turn it off.

### Speeds and units

- Speeds keep their unit in every analysis: mph recordings (or unlabelled ones with mph set) are coached and compared in mph, and the Corner Analyzer never subtracts km/h from mph.

### Polish

- Arek's review of the whole translation is applied: *odcinek*, *zapis*, *teoretyczny czas okrążenia*, *jazda bez gazu i hamulca*, *łączne przeciążenie*, *hamowanie*.
- The circuit dialog's title is just the session (for example *Sesja 1*).

### Easier to read and use

- Day page: a one-session day says why time losses compare nothing; a failed coach or Car/Driver summary explains why and offers *Calculate again*; tappable rows are read as buttons by screen readers.
- Screen readers read each sector table row as one line, hear the segment timing strip, and get each chart's lowest and highest value; the G-G panel says why it is empty.
- On large windows, detail pages, the day summary and the import review stay at most 840 wide; the lap picker is narrower.
- On a Mac or PC: Tab moves through one pane at a time, chart cursors follow the mouse, Ctrl/Cmd+S saves the day, and Enter confirms excluding a lap.

### More reliable

- Saving first writes any waiting recovery changes.
- macOS: a save cut short by a crash or power loss is finished the next time the day is opened.
