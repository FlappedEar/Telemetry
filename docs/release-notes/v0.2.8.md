## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.8 (pre-release)

A Sunlight look for reading the app outdoors, and a screen that stays on while Coach is shown. It installs over 0.2.7 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Look

- **Settings › Look** offers **Dark** (the default, unchanged) and **Sunlight**: black text on white, deeper colours for text, buttons and tabs, and darker loss, gain and best colours. Your lap stays amber and the reference lap blue. Chart lines are thicker, and the track map keeps its speed colours visible on white. The choice is kept.

### Coach between sessions

- While **Coach** is shown, and on pages opened from it, the screen stays on while the app is in front, so the Next session card is still there when you come back to the phone. Elsewhere in the app, and when it is not in front, the screen sleeps as usual. It works the same on a Mac. This is on by default; turn off **Keep the screen on while Coach is shown** in Settings to let it sleep on Coach too.

### Install

Android: the `.apk` below. macOS 12 or later: the `-macos.zip` below; it is not notarized; the install page says how to open it the first time. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
