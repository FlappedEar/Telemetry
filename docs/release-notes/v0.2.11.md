## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.11 (pre-release)

Share the day report as one picture, a map that says when its background needs a connection, saving with Ctrl or Cmd+S from a lap's page, and the places at the side on a phone held sideways or a tablet held upright. It installs over 0.2.10 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Sharing the report

- **Share as image**, under the group's name on the day's **Report** tab, turns the whole report into one picture (PNG), headed by the day's name and followed by the coach's **Next session** notes, without buttons or maps, in the look and language the app uses. On a phone or tablet the share sheet opens; on a computer you choose where to save it, and the app says *Report saved as an image.* The button waits while a part of the report or the coach is still being calculated. If the picture cannot be made, for example when the report is too long for one image, the app says why.

### Maps without signal

- When the street or satellite background cannot load, the map says *The map background needs a connection; the trace is drawn without it.* in its bottom-left corner while any part of the background is missing; move or reopen the map when the signal is back. Apple Maps shows no such note.

### Lap and comparison pages

- **Ctrl+S** or **Cmd+S** now saves the day from a lap's page and the Corner Analyzer too, not only from the day page.
- On a wide but short window, a lap's page stays in a centred column instead of stretching across the whole width.
- Moving the cursor in the Corner Analyzer redraws only the cursor, not the whole chart. The chart looks the same.

### Phones held sideways and tablets

- From 600 points of width, such as a phone held sideways or a tablet held upright, **Home**, **Library**, **Day**, **Coach** and **Profile** are in a rail at the side instead of a bar at the bottom, so the page keeps its height. The rail scrolls when the screen is too short for all of them. A phone held upright keeps the bar at the bottom.

### Install

Android: the `.apk` below. macOS 12 or later: the `-macos.zip` below; it is not notarized; the install page says how to open it the first time. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
