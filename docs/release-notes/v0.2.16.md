## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.16 (pre-release)

**Coach** now opens with a short summary of the session recorded last, and a lap that cannot be a real lap of the circuit is no longer ranked, so it can no longer be the best lap. It installs over 0.2.15 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### The session in 30 seconds

- At the top of **Coach**, a new card, for example **Session 6 in 30 seconds**, sums up the session recorded last (the one the **Next session** card coaches) in a few lines:
  - **Best lap**: its time and whether it is a new best of the day; otherwise how far it is from the quickest best lap of the other sessions, or that it equals it, naming that session.
  - **Lap-time spread**: how repeatable the lap times were, next to the session before's. It needs at least 3 ranked laps.
  - **Biggest gain** and **Biggest loss**: the segments whose typical time changed the most since the session before.
  - **Biggest gap left**: the segment furthest from the quickest typical time of any session there.
  - **Car, hottest**: the highest value of each temperature the recording has (for example oil or coolant), next to the session before's.
  - **Focus from** the session before: how this session did on the coach's main focus.
- Every number comes from results the day already shows elsewhere; nothing is measured differently, and they are observations from your laps, not causes. "The session before" is the latest earlier session with a ranked lap. A segment needs at least 3 laps through it, and a change or gap under 0.05 s is not counted. The segment lines wait for the theoretical best.
- After a session is added, the page moves to **Coach** with this card at the top and the **Next session** card below it.

### Implausible laps

- A lap is marked **Lap time or length is not plausible for this circuit** when it is shorter than 3 s or longer than an hour, its GPS path is shorter than 200 m or its average speed is above 360 km/h. It is also marked when its GPS path is shorter than 80% of the typical lap of its session (when the session has at least two laps to compare) or, when the day has at least two recordings on the same circuit, of the typical lap across them. This is usually a false crossing of the start/finish line.
- Such a lap stays in the lap list with that reason, but it is never ranked, never the best lap and not used for statistics or as a reference. Like the other laps that are not ranked, it is hidden unless you choose **Show N laps not ranked** above the list.
- Laps on normal days are unchanged. Saved days get this check when they are next opened. Overlays has no such check, so it may still rank these laps.
