## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.20 (pre-release)

Each track now has a notebook in your driver profile: notes, a note for each corner and things to try next time. It installs over 0.2.19 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Track notebook

- Each track has a notebook for the next visit. Open it with the book button next to the track in the library, or as **Track notebook** from the **⋮** menu of a saved day at that track. Every day at that track shows the same notebook, and it is saved as you type.
- **To try**: things to try next time. Type one and tap **+**; tick it when it is done and it moves under **Done**; the bin removes it. A notebook holds up to 256 things to try.
- **Notes**: anything about the track, such as grip, bumps or where to brake.
- **Corners**: one note for each of the track's corners.
- **Import profile** combines the notebooks of a track both devices have, even when no day is added: notes and things to try from the file are added to yours, and nothing of yours is replaced. Whatever does not fit is left out, and the import says so.

### Combining recordings

- A channel taken from the other recording of a session (for example an RCZ combined with a VBO) now stays within the time span of the session's own recording. Before, it could reach before the session's start or past its end. A channel with nothing left inside the span is no longer added or counted in *channels added*. Lap times do not change. Overlays still keeps the whole channel.
