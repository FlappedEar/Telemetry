## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.24 (pre-release)

Each corner now says what kind of corner it is: how it is braked for, how fast it is, and its shape. It installs over 0.2.23 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Corner type

- At the bottom of the corner details, **Corner type** describes the corner in three parts, and the same short line is on the corner's row in **Where the time goes**. It describes the corner, not one lap, so it is the same for every lap you pick.
- **Braking**, from this day's laps: **Heavy braking** (braking on at least half of the laps and typically at least 40 km/h slower at the lowest point than where braking starts), **Braking**, **Braking (heavy not known)**, **Lift** or **Flat**. Braking points come from the brake pedal or are inferred from deceleration, never both together, and the corner details say which.
- **Speed**, from this day's laps: **Slow corner** (typical minimum speed below 80 km/h), **Medium-speed corner** (80 to 130 km/h) or **Fast corner** (from 130 km/h). Speeds in different units are never mixed.
- **Shape**, from the track: **Complex**, **Double apex**, **Decreasing radius**, **Increasing radius**, **Late apex** or **Single apex**.
- The limits are the same on every track. Braking and speed need at least three laps; in the corner details a part that cannot be told says why (the row in Where the time goes leaves it out). A segment holding several corners, such as an automatic *Corners 9–16*, is classed as a whole, usually as *Double apex* or *Complex*. A segment across the start/finish line gets its shape only, with no braking or speed class.
