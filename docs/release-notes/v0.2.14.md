## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.14 (pre-release)

Circuits are now named from GPS, and Earlier visits here names the corner the day's figures are kept on, for days added from this version on. It installs over 0.2.13 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Circuit names

- The app names a circuit from the session's GPS: where the route's start/finish line lies within about 2 km of the centre of a circuit in its list, the route takes that circuit's name. The name shows in the Circuits rows, the group line under the best lap (for example *Group 1 · Autodrom Jastrząb · Counterclockwise*), the day report and, for a new track, your library (a second layout there becomes *Autodrom Jastrząb 2*; tracks you already have keep their names). Where no circuit is known, it still says *Detected route*, and new tracks are still called *Track N*.
- In the **Circuit of Session N** dialog a line says which circuit was found, or that the place is not in the circuit list. **Name this circuit…** gives it your own name, used for every day driven there, or adds a circuit the list does not have; your names are kept on this device and do not change how laps are grouped.
- **Settings › Circuits** shows how many circuits the list holds, lists **Your circuit names** (the bin forgets one; a renamed circuit gets its listed name back) and has **Update the circuit list**. The list ships with the app and is fetched from GitHub when the app starts, at most once a day, while **Check for a new version when the app starts** is on; with it off, the list stays as it is until you tap **Update the circuit list**. Nothing about your sessions is sent. Circuit names and positions come from Wikidata (CC0), so a name can be in the circuit's own language.

### Earlier visits here

- For a day added from this version on, the **Earlier visits here** line under each area in **Where to look next** names the corner the day's figures are kept on in your library. Before, when a later day added a corner that fitted the place better, the line for an older day could name that neighbouring corner instead.
- Days added from this version on keep which of the day's segments each corner was placed from. Days added before keep being matched as in 0.2.13, and can still name a neighbouring corner, until they are added again in this version.

### Driver profile files

- A `.feprofile` exported from this version keeps, for each corner of a session, the day's segment it was placed from. Earlier versions open these files, ignore the new field and keep it when they save, unless they measure the day's corners again.

### Driving states

- In **Driving states** on the lap comparison, the distance shown for **Braking while cornering** no longer counts where the recording's speed has a gap (two samples more than three times its usual interval apart), as in FlappedEar Overlays. The time and the strips are unchanged, and recordings without such gaps show the same distance as before.
