## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.12 (pre-release)

Last time here now compares each corner with your previous visit. It installs over 0.2.11 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Last time here, corner by corner

- Under **Best lap** and **Theoretical best**, the **Last time here** card on the day's **Overview** now lists **Time lost per corner**: each of the track's corners measured on both days, by the name it has in your library, with how far that day's laps typically were from that day's fastest time through the corner, averaged over its sessions, last time and today, and the difference.
- Each day is measured against its own best in that corner, so a smaller time means a more even day there, not a faster corner. Where a corner starts and ends can also differ a little between days. The card says so. A corner measured on only one of the days is not listed, and when no corner was measured on both (an older day, or today's corners still being worked out), the card says that too.
