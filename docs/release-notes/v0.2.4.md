## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.4 (pre-release)

Home, Library and Day on every page, a tidier lap list, and a coach that checks how the last main focus went. It installs over 0.2.3 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### Easier to get around

- **Home, Library and Day** stay in the bar at the bottom (or the rail at the side on a wide screen) on every page.
- The day page has tabs under the day's name: **Day**, **Laps** and **Compare**. On a wide screen the laps sit beside the analysis, so the tabs are Day and Compare.
- The lap list hides laps that are not ranked (out and in laps, laps without a start/finish pass, excluded laps and laps with an issue). **Show N laps not ranked** brings them back, and the app remembers your choice. The day and its recordings keep every lap.

### A better coach

- **Main focus from Session N** checks the main focus the coach gave for the session before: the value then, the value in this session, and Better, About the same or Worse. It says Better or Worse only for a clear change, and it is an observation, not proof that the advice worked.
- A new tip, **Pick up the throttle once**, appears when the throttle is picked up after braking and released again before the slow point, while your faster laps pick it up once.
- **Why?** marks this session's point (amber) and your faster laps' point (blue) on the corner's map for a lift, a braking start, a throttle return or a first throttle pickup.
- **Why?** also shows the mean combined G through the corner, and the highest on this session's laps against the highest today. It needs both accelerations, so a RaceChrono RCZ import shows none.

### More reliable

- Combining a VBO and an RCZ: a channel only one recording gives a unit for is combined only when the values agree, so far more channels are combined on a typical day.
- Saves stay compatible with Overlays' recovery, so Overlays keeps its own unsaved edits after a Telemetry save.
