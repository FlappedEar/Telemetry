## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.1 (pre-release)

Fixes and polish on top of 0.2.0. It installs over 0.2.0, over 0.1.0 and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### Easier to read

- **Large text and large screens.** With very large text on a phone, lap rows, the lap legend, the corner sheet, time losses, coasting, the lap picker, Compare and the segment editor now wrap instead of running off the screen. On a tablet or desktop the day, import, report, diagnostics and settings pages keep a readable width.
- **Units stay with their numbers**, so a wrapped line never starts with "s" or "km/h".
- **Calmer colours:** the start/finish gate is black on a white edge, and the progression median, coach label and Corner Analyzer cursor are neutral instead of amber or green.
- **Polish for recording errors and notes.** About 80 messages about a recording (a damaged file, missing GPS, laps that cannot be timed, combining VBO and RCZ) were still English; they are now Polish too.

### Easier to use

- **No best lap? Set the circuit.** A day without a best lap offers *Set the circuit…*, and a session whose circuit is unknown opens its circuit dialog.
- **Cancel a long clock check or primary change** on a combined VBO + RCZ session.
- A recording without GPS positions now says so, instead of only showing no laps.
- A failed or cancelled import is a banner that says what to do next.
- A failed theoretical best is shown once, with one *Calculate again*.
- On a phone, Settings is in the *More* menu so the day's title has room.

### More reliable

- A day can be left as soon as a waiting save has lined up its recordings, while the file is still being written.

### Install

Android: the `.apk` below. macOS 12 or later: the `-macos.zip` below; it is not notarized; the install page says how to open it the first time. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
