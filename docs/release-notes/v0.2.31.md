## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.31 (pre-release)

The library can now read all your saved days again, so figures that a newer version measures or measures differently apply to every day at once, and linked corners are now measured one by one. It installs over 0.2.30 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Recalculate all days

- On the **Library** page, tap ⋮ and **Recalculate all days**. Every day is read again from its recordings, one at a time, while the bar under the title counts them. It carries on if you leave the page, and **Stop** ends it after the day being read.
- Your skill levels on the profile are worked out from the figures stored for each session every time the profile is shown, so a change to how levels are set applies to every day at once.
- Cars, names and weather stay as they are, and a day stays on its track while its route matches it, as when you open it.
- When it is done the app says *n days recalculated*. A day whose file or one of whose recordings is missing or unreadable keeps what was measured before, and the message says how many.
- A day saved before the app measured distance and time gets them this way too, or when you open it. Until then, the profile says how many sessions its distance and time cover.

### Corners are measured one by one

- Corners with no straight of 20 m or more between them (an S-bend, a chicane, a run of linked corners) are now proposed one by one, each with its own number, so the advice names one corner. A stretch that used to come as *Corners 9–16* now comes as *Corner 9*, *Corner 10* and so on up to *Corner 16*; the corners after it keep their numbers.
- In the segment review, the boundary between two such corners is marked as uncertain.
- A day saved by an earlier version keeps the segments it has, and so do segments you moved, split or merged. Their corners keep being classed as a whole, as before.
- The theoretical best and the corner figures for a new day follow the finer corners, so they can differ from an earlier version's for the same laps.

### Profile export on the Mac

- **Export profile** now writes straight into the file you chose, and a recording the system will not let the app read is left out like one that is no longer on the device, instead of stopping the export. When the file still cannot be written, the app says *The profile could not be exported:* followed by the reason the system gives.
- The cause of the failure seen on the Mac is not confirmed: this build is meant to fix it or at least show why.

### Also in this build

- In the Polish app, the texts of three cards are reworded: *Optymalne fazy zakrętów* (best phases), *Technika hamowania* (braking technique) and *Style jazdy* (lap styles).
- The user guide is now also in Polish. On the guide site, use the language switch at the top of any page; the Polish pages are under /pl/.
