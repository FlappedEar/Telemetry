## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.5 (pre-release)

A Profile that follows you across every day: totals, records per track, corners that keep costing time, and 12 skills with a level from 1 to 5. It installs over 0.2.4 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### Your Profile

- **Profile** is a new place beside Home, Library, Day and Coach.
- **All days** adds up your days, sessions, timed laps, distance and time on track, tracks and cars. Days saved before this version add their distance and time, theoretical best, typical laps, corners and skills when you open them again.
- **Tracks** shows each track's best lap, best theoretical and best typical lap, and whether your best lap on the last visit was faster or slower than on the visit before.
- **Corners that keep costing time** lists corners that were among a visit's costliest on two visits or more. Each one is marked as still costing time, fading or no longer lost.
- Each **car** shows how many days you drove it, with distance and time.
- A track's corners stay in the same places on every visit, so a corner is compared with itself across days.

### Skills

- **Skills** rates 12 skills over your last 3 days that measured each one, against fixed bands. Each shows **Level N of 5** (5 is the best), the measured value, a confidence based on the ranked laps behind it, and, once there are earlier days to compare with, a trend: Improving, Steady or Declining.
- Four skills are measured so far: brake point consistency, minimum speed control, exit speed execution and pace consistency. Until a day measures one, it says **Needs more evidence**. The other eight say **Not measured by the app yet**.
- **Coach** shows the same skills below the Next session card.
