## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.26 (pre-release)

You can now compare your laps with a **reference lap** from outside the day, such as a friend's or an instructor's recording or one of your earlier days. It installs over 0.2.25 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Reference lap

- A reference lap stays apart from the day: it is not ranked, not part of the theoretical best, the progression or the coach, and it is not saved with the day. It is kept while the day is open; open the day again and it is gone.
- At the end of the **Compare** tab, **Load a recording** opens the file picker: choose one VBO or RaceChrono RCZ file (if you pick several, only the first is used and the app says so). **From an earlier day** lists the other days of your profile.
- The reference is timed on today's start/finish line, so a VBO and an RCZ work against each other. Its fastest complete lap is the reference; **Choose its lap** picks another. A lap its own day excluded is marked *Excluded on its day* and is never taken as the fastest, but you can still choose it.
- A recording that cannot be used says why: another track (its GPS never comes within 500 m of today's start/finish line), laps driven the other way or round another layout (checked when today's best lap has a route to compare with; otherwise only the start/finish line is checked and the app says so), no completed lap across the line, no GPS, or a file that cannot be read.
- If today's line changes, for example when you show another group, the reference says it was timed on another line and compares nothing until you tap **Load again**. **Clear reference** drops it.
- **Against the reference**: today's best lap (A) against the reference, with the lap time difference, today's segments timed on both laps, and speed, throttle and brake by track position. A positive difference means A is behind. The lap difference is hidden when the reference follows less than 97 % of lap A's line. Speeds in different units are drawn as two charts and never subtracted.

### Already in 0.2.25: fixes a few metres off the line

- 0.2.25 also included this fix, which its notes left out. When following a lap along the track, a GPS fix that lies a few metres off the line is now kept when no other part of the track is near and it does not lie well inside a bend, instead of being refused, which could break a lap's position along the track into pieces and leave gaps in the lap. Fixes that could belong to another part of the track, such as the other leg of a hairpin, are still refused. Its lap times and theoretical best on the real day it was tested on are unchanged.
