## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.5.1 (pre-release)

This release makes the app refuse VBO files whose columns cannot be matched to their values with certainty, places the corners kept in your driver profile more exactly, and tries again to save a circuit list it could not save. It installs over 0.5.0 and earlier releases and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Recordings

- A VBO file whose column names and row values do not match is no longer read with a guessed clock. This holds when most rows have fewer values than the header has names and the time column is not the first one (a name written with a space, such as `UTC time`, counts as two), and when most rows have more values than there are names (an unnamed column could stand before the time). Such a file is listed as one that could not be read, with the reason in English and Polish. Empty fields from trailing separators do not count, a file with a time column first and short rows is still read, and your six real sessions import as before.
- Known limit: a header that lists trailing names the rows never fill is also refused, because that cannot be told from a name split in two.

### Driver profile

- The corners kept in your driver profile are placed by the distance along the lap instead of by an even spacing of the points. On the real Jastrząb day the positions move by at most 2.0 m on VBO sessions and 13.9 m on RCZ sessions; lap times, laps and segments are unchanged (25 laps, best 1:49.898). A profile corner shorter than about 28 m on a day timed from an RCZ could be stored twice after the day is analysed again; nothing migrates this yet.

### Circuits

- When a newer circuit list is fetched but cannot be saved on the device, the app now uses it until you close the app and tries to save it again at the next refresh. Before, it called the list up to date and did not try again.
