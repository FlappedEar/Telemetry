## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.18 (pre-release)

Braking is now worked out from deceleration when the brake channel does not show it, for example with an unplugged brake sensor, as long as the session records longitudinal G. **Before you go out** now leads with your lap times and also shows the car. It installs over 0.2.17 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Braking when the brake channel does not show it

- Before, a session with a brake channel always used it, even one with no data, so with an unplugged brake sensor no braking points were found at the corners and no braking time was counted.
- Now the brake channel is used only when it shows the braking. When it has (almost) no data, or is not pressed in at least half of the hard brakings the longitudinal G shows (three or more are needed to judge), the session's braking points at the corners and its braking and coasting time are worked out from deceleration instead and marked *inferred*. This needs a longitudinal G in g (or with no unit) that shows at least light braking; without one, the brake channel is used as before. The corner details on the day page say *Inferred from deceleration: the brake channel does not show the braking*, and the coach does not count the brake as a recorded pedal. The brake trace on the charts and in **Compare** still shows the brake channel.
- A brake recorded in a unit other than % (a brake with no unit counts as %) is not replaced unless it has (almost) no data; it still shows *channel unit not supported*. Overlays still uses the brake channel whenever there is one, so it can show no braking where this version shows inferred braking.

### Before you go out

- The page now opens with **Last ranked lap**, **Best of the day** and **To the best** in large digits: the last ranked lap of the session recorded last on the circuit shown, the day's best lap there, and the difference. A lap that is not ranked, for example an excluded, off-route or short lap, is skipped, so it can be an earlier lap; the lap name under the time says which.
- It now shows every point of the **Next session** card in its order: at most two changes and one thing to keep, three in all. Before, it showed only the first two changes, and the thing to keep only when there was no change. A new **Car** line shows the session's hottest temperatures and the summary's *Car, last laps* lines.
