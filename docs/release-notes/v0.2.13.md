## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.13 (pre-release)

Where to look next now says how each corner went on your earlier visits. It installs over 0.2.12 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Earlier visits here

- For a day kept in your library, each area in **Where to look next** gets an **Earlier visits here** line under its hypothesis. It comes from your earlier days at this track in this car; a later day, today and other cars do not count. An undated day counts every dated day as earlier.
- When the area's segment is one of the track's corners in your library, the line names that corner and says on how many of the visits that measured it the corner cost time, with the date of the last one, for example *Earlier visits here (Turn 4): it cost time on 2 of 3 visits, last on Aug 29, 2026.* A corner costs time on a visit when it is among that day's three costliest corners by 0.1 s or more, the same rule as **Corners that keep costing time**.
- If the last two visits that measured the corner lost no time there, the line says so. A corner that was measured but never among the costliest says that too.
- Otherwise the line says why there is nothing to show: no earlier day here in this car, the corner was not measured before, or the place is not one of the track's corners in your library yet (a straight, for example). It is an observation, not a cause. A day whose track was not recognised, or whose corners could not be placed on the ground (no usable GPS), shows no line, as with **Last time here**.
