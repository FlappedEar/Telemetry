## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.29 (pre-release)

The day report now has a **Lap styles** card: it groups the day's ranked laps by how they were braked and driven on the throttle, and shows the best lap of each group. It installs over 0.2.28 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Lap styles

- The card sits below **Grip and balance**. It is closed at first, with one line; tap **Show** to open it and **Hide** to close it again. It is labelled **Inferred**: the groups come from figures the Corner Analyzer already measures, not from a new measurement.
- Out laps, in laps, laps you excluded and laps with issues are not grouped; the card says how many of the compared group's timed laps are grouped.
- Each lap is compared with the day's typical lap at each corner where it has a figure (the median of the day's laps, from at least 3 laps measured the same way) on braking point, throttle pickup, minimum speed and exit speed. The braking point is read from the longitudinal acceleration, or from the speed's slope when there is none, never from the brake pedal. The card says when a channel without a unit is read as g, km/h or mph.
- A lap's group comes from plain counting rules over the corners where it has a figure: **Conservative** (braking earlier and the throttle later than typical, each in most corners), **Late braking**, **Early throttle**, **Mixed**, **Typical** and **Outlier**. Speeds are shown for comparison but do not decide a group, because they follow a lap's pace.
- Tap a group to see its laps, its best lap and how far that is from the day's best lap, and how many of its laps are among the quicker half of the day. Under that, its best lap is set against the day's typical: at how many corners it braked earlier or later, picked up the throttle earlier or later, and was faster or slower. Tap a lap in the list to open it.
- The groups are observations, not causes: a session has few laps, and laps also quicken as the driver learns the track, so a group mixes style with progress. A day with fewer than 3 ranked laps, or with fewer than 3 corners where a braking or throttle point can be compared, says so and shows no groups.
