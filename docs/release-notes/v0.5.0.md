## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.5.0 (pre-release)

This release keeps your choices when a day is opened or grows: a refused combination stays refused, and a circuit's kept corners and names no longer disappear when a recording is added. It installs over 0.3.1 and earlier releases and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Days that grow

- When you add recordings to a day one by one, the circuit keeps the identity it was first given. Before, adding a recording could rename the circuit (on the Jastrząb day, when the fourth session was added), and the corners you had kept and named for it were measured again or dropped. Adding the six real sessions one at a time now keeps one circuit. A day already renamed by the earlier behaviour does not get its lost corners back.
- If two circuits merge into one when a recording is added, or one splits, the corners kept for the other are still not carried over. The app does not yet say so; this is tracked as FET-267.

### Combining a VBO with its RCZ

- If you refuse to combine a session with its RCZ (**Refuse** in the clock check, or **Don't combine**), the refusal is saved with the day. Opening the day again no longer lines the RCZ up and combines it again; it stays beside the session, and **Check clock** still works if you change your mind. Making the RCZ the primary recording behaves as before. The row no longer says the choice lasts only until the day is opened again, and the user guide (English and Polish) says the same.
- The saved day gains an optional `fusionDeclined` key on the session. Telemetry and Overlays no longer need to stay compatible (owner decision of 8 October 2026), so Overlays is not checked against it.

### Android

- Copies of recordings that were made while importing and that no saved day or unsaved-day recovery uses (a duplicate, a rejected file, a cancelled import) were removed only when the app started. They are now also removed when you bring the app back to the front, at most once an hour, only while the import page is shown, and only for copies more than a day old. The rest of FET-297 (removal the moment an import ends) is not in this release.
