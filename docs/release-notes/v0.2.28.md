## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.28 (pre-release)

Each corner's details now show **Braking technique**: how hard and how quickly you brake into the corner, lap by lap and typically over the day. It installs over 0.2.27 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Braking technique

- In the corner details, under **Corner type**, each line shows *This lap …* next to the typical value over the day's ranked laps. Typical is the median of at least three laps that braked there.
- **Initial hit** is how fast the deceleration builds, in g per second. **Peak deceleration** is the highest deceleration in g, with how many seconds into the braking it comes. **Release** is how fast the deceleration falls off into the apex. **Trail braking** is how long, and for this lap how many metres, braking goes on while the lateral G is at least 0.3 g; it is inferred, as there is no steering channel. **Brake to throttle** is the time from the end of braking to the throttle pickup, shown only when the recording has a throttle channel. **Brake pedal: application and release** is how fast the pedal is pressed and let off, in % per second, from a brake channel of about 10 Hz or faster.
- The deceleration comes from the longitudinal G channel when the recording has one, otherwise from the change of the speed, and the line under the title says which. Laps measured from G and from speed, or in different units, are never mixed. From speed, a figure quicker than the smoothing can show reads *at least*, and a brake-to-throttle time from a throttle slower than about 10 Hz reads *about*.
- A channel without a unit is read as g, km/h (or mph, when the setting for speeds without a unit says so) or %, and the section says so. A figure that cannot be told says why, for example *This lap: not known, braking peaks below 0.40 g*; when the whole corner cannot be measured, the section says *Not known: …*. When a typical rests on fewer braking laps than most, it says *from n of m braking laps*.
- The theoretical best card has a one-line **Braking technique** summary of the day under the sector table: the typical hit, release, trail braking and brake-to-throttle over the corners braked into. Corners measured another way are left out and counted, and a corner whose figure rests on under half of its braking laps is left out of that figure. Tap a corner in **Where the time goes** for its braking lap by lap.
- A segment holding several corners, such as *Corners 5–6*, is measured on its first braking.
