## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.17 (pre-release)

You can now set your own goals for the next session, the session summary checks them and reads the car over the last laps, **Before you go out** gathers what to work on in large type, and you can remove a session from a day or delete a day from the library. When a VBO and an RCZ of one session are combined, more disagreements between them are now shown, and **Fill gaps** and **Use RCZ** can now also fill stretches where a recording lists times but has no values. A VBO file without a time column is no longer read. It installs over 0.2.16 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Your own goals for the next session

- At the bottom of the **Next session** card on **Coach**, **Add a goal** sets up to three goals of your own for the next run, each a change at one of today's corners, for example *Corner 3 · Reduce coasting*. The changes are a later lift, less coasting, more speed through the slow point, an earlier throttle return, picking up the throttle once, or braking at the same point every lap. The cross next to a goal removes it.
- Goals can be set once the theoretical best is ready and the session has laps among the compared laps. The first session of a day has no goals to check, because goals are set after one session for the next. They are kept with the session they were set after and saved with the day, so save the day to keep them.
- While the next session is the latest one, the session summary shows a **Your goal** line for each one and checks it the way the coach checks its main focus: *Better* or *Worse* only for a clear change, otherwise *About the same*. When a goal cannot be measured, the line says so, for example when today's corners no longer include that corner.
- FlappedEar Overlays keeps the goals in the day file without showing them. Goals saved by a newer version are shown but cannot be edited here.

### Car over the last laps

- The session summary on **Coach** has a **Car, last laps** line, read from the session's ranked laps only, so the pit lane, cooling down or a detour do not read as the car:
  - *Still rising*: a temperature whose highest value on a lap rose 8 °C or more over the last 3 ranked laps and did not drop on the last one, for example *Oil still rising: 115 → 128 (laps 1–3)*.
  - *Strong acceleration lower*: when strong acceleration (the 90th percentile of forward G on a lap) on the last ranked lap is 5% or more below the session's highest, once at least 4 ranked laps have it, with the temperature that rose most over the same laps when one rose 2 °C or more. Traffic and a different line lower it too, and the line says so.
- When a part cannot be read, it says why. A part the session did not record is left out. These are observations from the recording, not a diagnosis.

### Before you go out

- **Before you go out**, beside the title of the session summary on **Coach**, opens a page to read at the car in large type, from the session recorded last: the **Main focus** from the **Next session** card with what was measured (when there is nothing to change, **Keep** and what to keep doing, or why the coach gave nothing), the card's second change under **Once that feels settled** when there is one, **Your goals**, and the summary's biggest gap left as **Biggest chance**.
- Nothing is measured differently there; when a line cannot be filled it says why, as on the cards.

### Removing a session or a day

- **Remove session** in a session's **Details**, or **More** (⋮) › **Remove a session…** on the day, takes a session out of the day after asking. The day is saved and opens again without it, and the best lap, lap list, theoretical best, coach and library are worked out again. Laps of it you excluded, and comparisons that used its laps, go with it. If the day's corners were measured on that session, they are measured again on the best lap left and names you gave them are lost; the dialog says when this may happen. The recording file is not deleted. **Undo** in the message right after puts the session back, while the message shows and the day has not changed since. A day keeps at least one session.
- In the **Library**, ⋮ on a day › **Delete day** deletes it after asking, because this cannot be undone. Its sessions leave your profile, and copies of recordings the app made for it are deleted when no other day uses them; your own recording files are never deleted. **Change car** has moved into the same ⋮ menu.

### Reading recordings

- A VBO file without a time column (`time`, `timestamp` or `utc_time`) is no longer read; the import says why. Before, its rows were timed one second apart, as Overlays still does, which gave believable but wrong speeds and lap times. A saved day with such a recording still opens, with that session listed among the sessions that could not be opened and the reason; **More** (⋮) › **Remove a session…** takes it out of the day.

### Combining a VBO and an RCZ

- A recording now counts as having no data wherever it has no value for a channel, even where its file lists times for it. Before, such a stretch counted as covered, so **Fill gaps** (the VBO, and the RCZ where the VBO has no data) and **Use RCZ** (the RCZ, and the VBO where the RCZ has no data) left it empty; now the other recording fills it where it has readings inside that stretch.
- A stretch neither recording has values for still stays a gap, and so does a short stretch the other recording has no reading inside, for example when it records less often: a value is never read across a recording's own missing values. **Keep VBO**, the default, is unchanged.
- Two recordings of a channel now also count as disagreeing when they are more than three times the usual limit apart on more than one sample in ten, over everything both recorded or only where the channel is in use, such as the brake pressed or the throttle open. Before, only their typical difference counted, so a brake that reads the same at rest but differently in every braking zone counted as agreeing, with no row; now it gets the row with **Keep VBO**, **Fill gaps** and **Use RCZ**.
- A VBO file names no units. As before, a VBO channel whose typical difference from the RCZ is over the usual limit is set aside as a different unit: the VBO's values are kept and no row is shown. A channel that disagrees only in the new ways is set aside the same way only when the two differ by the factor between two units of that kind of value (for example 0 to 1 against 0 to 100) and agree once rescaled. Any other, such as a VBO brake that reads nothing or half the RCZ's while braking, used to count as agreeing and is now shown with the three buttons.
- Overlays still treats a stretch with times but no values as covered, so it can show a gap there where this version shows the other recording's values. It also still counts two recordings as disagreeing only by their typical difference.
