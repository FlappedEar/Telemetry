## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.7 (pre-release)

Take your library to another device, sessions shared from RaceChrono that land in today's day, and segments that are measured again when a new best lap takes a line they cannot follow. It installs over 0.2.6 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Library

- **Library › ⋮ › Export profile** writes one `.feprofile` file with your profile, every saved day and the recordings they use. On a Mac or PC you choose where to save it; on a phone or tablet the share sheet opens (AirDrop, e-mail, a cloud drive). A recording no longer on the device is left out, and the message says how many. Changes not yet saved are not in the file.
- **Library › ⋮ › Import profile** adds the file's days that the library does not have yet, with their recordings. Importing never replaces anything: a day already there stays as it is, a car with the same name and a track with the same circuit join the ones already there, so your history at a track continues. A damaged file, a file that is not an exported profile, or one from a newer version of the app is refused and nothing changes.

### Importing during the day

- With no day open, a recording you share from RaceChrono (or pick) now tries the days saved in the last 24 hours, newest first (up to three), until one takes it. Before, only the newest saved day was tried, so after opening and saving an earlier visit's day between sessions, today's sessions could split into two days.

### Segments

- When you add a session whose best lap took a line the segments cannot follow, so some segments got no time, the segments are now measured again on that lap, and the day has them as changes to save. A name you gave a segment carries over to the new segment of the same kind covering the same part of the lap. This happens only while the segments are the automatic ones (renamed or not); segments you moved, split, merged or removed stay as they are, and opening a saved day never measures again.
