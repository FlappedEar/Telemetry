## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.15 (pre-release)

Last time here now compares the weather and setup of your previous visit with today's, skill levels are stricter, and each session can keep its setup. It installs over 0.2.14 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Weather in Last time here

- The **Last time here** card on the day's **Overview** has a **Weather** part between the times and the corners, with a line for each visit, for example *Then (Session 3, best lap): 19–22 °C, overcast, no rain, wind SW 12 km/h*.
- Each visit is shown by the session that set its best lap. When that session has no weather, or no session matches the best lap, the first session with weather is used and the line says so.
- When a visit has no weather, its line says why: for the previous visit, weather lookup was off, its recordings have no time or position, or the day was added before the library kept weather (opening that day once brings it in, if its file has the weather); for today, the weather is still being looked up, lookup is off, the service could not be reached or had no data, the recording has no time or GPS position, the day has unsaved changes, or its weather was saved by a newer version of the app.
- The card only shows weather your library already keeps and never looks weather up. Like the rest of the weather, it is a model's values for the area, not measured at the track.

### Stricter skill levels

- The skill levels in your driver profile and in the coach now use fixed bands set by what a fast, experienced driver repeats lap after lap, so level 5 of 5 means at the limit. Most bands are tighter than before, so your levels may drop without any change in your driving.
- Minimum speed, exit speed and braking effectiveness are now measured against your best ever at each corner in that car, not the same day's best, so a single faster lap there raises the bar for every day.
- A level also needs the laps to show it: level 5 needs 15 ranked laps; with 5 to 14 the level is at most 4, and with fewer at most 3. The trend compares the bands only, so more laps alone is no change.
- Corner sequence management is now averaged over the corners. The Skills intro on the Profile now says this too, and the new bands are listed in the user guide.

### Setup per session

- **Session details** has a **Setup** part, every field optional: tyre pressures in **bar** or **psi** for each wheel (**FL**, **FR**, **RL**, **RR**), **Cold** before the session and **Hot** after it, the **Tyres** used and **Fuel at start (l)** (0–200 l).
- Pressures are kept exactly as entered, in the unit you chose; switching between bar and psi never converts them, it only checks them against that unit's range (0.5–6 bar, 7–90 psi), and **Save** waits until a pressure out of range is fixed. A comma typed on a Polish keyboard becomes a decimal point.
- **Same as Session N** copies the setup of the session recorded just before, unit included, when it has one, asking first if fields already hold values. Nothing is kept until **Save**.
- The session's row in **Session details** and **Progression** show it on one line, for example *Setup: Cold 2.1 / 2.1 / 2 / 2 bar · Tyres Pirelli SC2 · Fuel 8.5 l*. The setup is saved in the day's file; FlappedEar Overlays keeps it there unchanged but does not show it. Clearing every field removes it; a setup saved by a newer version is shown but cannot be edited here.

### Setup in Last time here

- After the weather, the **Last time here** card has a **Setup** part with a line for each visit, exactly as entered, for example *Then (Session 3, best lap): Cold 2.1 / 2.1 / 2 / 2 bar · Tyres Pirelli SC2*. It picks the session the way the weather does: the one that set the best lap, else the first session with a setup, and the line says so.
- When both visits have pressures in the same unit, a third line gives today minus then for each wheel entered on both, for example *Difference (today − then): Cold +0.1 / 0 / −0.1 / — bar*. It has no colour: a higher or lower pressure is neither better nor worse. When one visit used bar and the other psi, both are shown as entered with *Different pressure units, not compared*.
- When a visit has no setup, its line says why. Your library gets a day's setup when the day is saved, so a setup you have just entered says so until the save.

### Lap comparison

- **Δ time (A − B)** now times each lap from its own start: where both laps are recorded up to the start/finish line, Δ time is 0 at the line and equals the **Lap Δ** at the finish, as in FlappedEar Overlays. The map's **Δ time** colouring uses the same timing, and a coasting episode starting at the line now reads 0 m.

### Lap detection

- A start/finish crossing now counts only when the GPS trace really goes over the line (or its continuation up to 5 m beyond either end), from one side to the other. Coming close to the line and leaving on the same side, such as stopping just before it or crossing it and turning back, no longer starts a new lap. Laps on days recorded normally are unchanged. FlappedEar Overlays still counts such passes.

### Settings and updates

- **Check for updates** is now also in the ⋮ menu on **Home**. In **Settings**, **Updates** is the first section and shows the version you have, and a scrollbar shows that the dialog scrolls.

### Driver profile files

- Your library now keeps a summary of each session's weather (temperature, weather, rain and wind) as it arrives, and a copy of each session's setup as the day was saved; a `.feprofile` exported from this version carries both. Earlier versions open these files and keep the weather and setup when they save, except for a day they add again (such as when it is opened), which loses both there; this version brings them back when that day is next opened: the setup if its file has it, the weather if its file has it or weather lookup is on.

### Combining a VBO and an RCZ

- When a VBO and an RCZ are combined, a stretch neither recording has stays a gap, also with **Fill gaps** or **Use RCZ** where the two record at different rates; before, the lap comparison's charts could bridge it and read figures from inside it.
