## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.30 (pre-release)

The reference lap you choose for a day is now remembered in your driver profile, so it is back when you open the day again. It installs over 0.2.29 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Reference lap remembered

- The reference you chose is kept in your driver profile, for that day only, and comes back when you open the day again. Under the reference it says *Remembered for this day in your driver profile* (for a recording, with *, with a copy of the recording*). A day that is not kept in your profile (a day saved as a file somewhere else) says *not remembered* instead: its reference lives while the day is open.
- For a recording (a friend's or an instructor's file), the app copies the file into your profile and remembers the copy, so it keeps working when the original is moved or deleted. Only the name of the file is shown; the place it was on your computer is never stored. A copy is kept once, however many days use it, and it travels with the profile when you export it and import it on another device; an import adds a day without its reference when the profile has no room for it.
- A recording larger than 128 MiB, or an RCZ or VBO the profile has no room for, cannot be remembered: the profile keeps at most 32 reference recordings (512 MiB together). The app says so under the reference, with **Try again**, and the reference still works while the day is open.
- For a lap of an earlier day, the profile remembers that day and the lap. It works while that day is in your profile; if you delete it, the reference says the day is no longer in your profile, and you can clear it or choose another.
- If the copy of a recording is missing from the profile, the day still opens and the reference says it was not found. If the profile cannot be written (the disk is full, say), the app says the reference is not remembered.
- **Clear reference** drops it, and the profile forgets it. Deleting a day deletes its reference with it, and the copy of its recording unless another day's reference or recording uses it.
- The reference is still not part of the day: it is not in the day's file and not in its ranking, theoretical best, progression or coach.
