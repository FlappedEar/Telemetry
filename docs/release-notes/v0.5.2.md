## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.5.2 (pre-release)

This release puts a circuit's VBO and RCZ sessions in one group, says why braking is inferred, tells you when saved segments no longer apply after the day's routes were regrouped, and reads a heading's unit from the recording's header. It installs over 0.5.1 and earlier releases and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Compared laps

- A day with some sessions as VBO and some as RCZ of the same circuit is now one group, so their laps are ranked together and the day has one best lap. Before, the two formats gave the start line slightly different values (the same line, its ends listed the other way round and a few billionths of a degree apart), and the app split the circuit into two groups. On the real Jastrząb day, sessions 1 to 3 as VBO and 4 to 6 as RCZ give one group with the best lap 1:49.898, the same as the day in either format alone.
- A day saved by an earlier version opens in the same group, with its kept corners, the proposals you rejected, your goals, the group shown, the laps you excluded and the pair you compared. They are moved to the new group when the day is opened, and the next save writes them so. Nothing is deleted.
- The saved day now records a new kind of start-line revision (`gates-v2:`). An earlier version refuses a day saved by this one as malformed, so keep a copy of a day if you may go back. Telemetry and Overlays no longer need to stay compatible (owner decision of 8 October 2026).
- Known limit: a start line whose centre lies right on the edge of the grid the revision rounds to can still be told apart between the two formats. The Jastrząb line is not near one.
- Known difference, not changed: on the real day, one lap (session 2, lap 3) is ranked when the session is read from its VBO and not when it is read from its RCZ.

### Analysis

- When a session has a brake channel the app cannot use, the driving panels, the coasting summary and the Corner Analyzer now say why braking is inferred: the brake channel does not show braking, or its scale is not known. Before, they said only that it was inferred. Corner details gets the same reasons. Nothing in your saved days changes.
- A heading, course or bearing channel is read in the unit the recording declares, including a unit written only on a VBO header line. A heading declared in radians is no longer unwrapped as degrees; it is charted as recorded with its unit. Degree headings, and your six real sessions, behave as before.

### Segments

- When the app regroups the day's routes (a group merges into another or splits), segments saved under the old group no longer apply. The Theoretical best card and the segment review now say how many saved segments are not used. Nothing is deleted or changed. The Polish wording of this note is a draft.
