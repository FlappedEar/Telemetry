## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.5.2 (pre-release)

This release says why braking is inferred, tells you when saved segments no longer apply after the day's routes were regrouped, and reads a heading's unit from the recording's header. It installs over 0.5.1 and earlier releases and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Analysis

- When a session has a brake channel the app cannot use, the driving panels, the coasting summary and the Corner Analyzer now say why braking is inferred: the brake channel does not show braking, or its scale is not known. Before, they said only that it was inferred. Corner details gets the same reasons. Nothing in your saved days changes.
- A heading, course or bearing channel is read in the unit the recording declares, including a unit written only on a VBO header line. A heading declared in radians is no longer unwrapped as degrees; it is charted as recorded with its unit. Degree headings, and your six real sessions, behave as before.

### Segments

- When the app regroups the day's routes (a group merges into another or splits), segments saved under the old group no longer apply. The Theoretical best card and the segment review now say how many saved segments are not used. Nothing is deleted or changed. The Polish wording of this note is a draft.
