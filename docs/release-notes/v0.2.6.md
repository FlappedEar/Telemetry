## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.6 (pre-release)

The app now checks for new versions itself, all 12 driver skills are measured, recorded channels can get your own names, every chart has a channel picker, the heading chart no longer jumps at north, and saving on a Mac or PC is safer. It installs over 0.2.5 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### Updates

- When it starts (at most once a day) and from **Settings › Check for updates**, the app asks GitHub for the newest release. If one is out, a message says so; tap **Details**. On Android, **Download and install** asks once for permission to install apps, downloads the APK, checks it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` and opens Android's installer. On a Mac, **Download** saves the zip where you choose, checks it and shows it in Finder; macOS refuses the first start of the new copy, as after the first install. On iPhone, iPad and Windows it only says that a newer version exists, and **What's new** opens the release on GitHub. To stop the check on launch, turn off **Check for a new version when the app starts** in Settings. Only requests for the releases and their files go to GitHub; none of your recordings or settings are sent.
- This is the first version with the check, so it can only find an update once a release after 0.2.6 is out.

### Driver profile

- All 12 skills on **Profile** and **Coach** now get a level from 1 to 5 with the measured value: the 8 that said "Not measured by the app yet" (lift timing, brake release timing, braking effectiveness, turn-in consistency, line consistency, throttle reapplication, throttle commitment, corner sequence management) are measured too. A skill whose channel a recording lacks (throttle, brake, longitudinal G, or a stated GPS accuracy) says **Needs more evidence**. A day saved with an earlier version gets the new measures when you open it again; until then those skills may say **Needs more evidence**.

### Coach

- When the laps, segments or group change, or the day is closed, while the coach is still preparing its plan, the old preparation stops at once instead of running to the end.

### Channels

- **Settings › Name channels** lists every recorded channel of the open day. A name you give there (for example Throttle for accelerator_pos-obd) is shown on charts, menus, the Car card and the day report, with the recorded name under it. Recordings and saved days keep the recorded names.
- **Star** the channels you chart most: the chart menus then list only those, with **All channels…** for the rest.
- Tap a channel chart's title to show another channel in its place.

### Charts

- The **heading** chart (and other compass channels in degrees) is drawn continuously across north instead of dropping from 360 to 0. The cursor still reads the recorded 0–360 value. In a comparison, lap B is turned to start within half a turn of lap A.

### Saving on a Mac or PC

- The Mac save dialog suggests the day's name with `.fetproject` once, not twice. On every device, a day named with `.fetproject` at the end no longer gets it twice.
- **Save as…** and **Export for Overlays…** ask **Replace <file>?** before replacing a file the save dialog did not warn about (on Windows and Linux). Cancel saves nothing.
