## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.6 (pre-release)

Your own names for recorded channels, a channel picker on every chart, a heading chart that no longer jumps at north, and safer saving on a Mac or PC. It installs over 0.2.5 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### Channels

- **Settings › Name channels** lists every recorded channel of the open day. A name you give there (for example Throttle for accelerator_pos-obd) is shown on charts, menus, the Car card and the day report, with the recorded name under it. Recordings and saved days keep the recorded names.
- **Star** the channels you chart most: the chart menus then list only those, with **All channels…** for the rest.
- Tap a channel chart's title to show another channel in its place.

### Charts

- The **heading** chart (and other compass channels in degrees) is drawn continuously across north instead of dropping from 360 to 0. The cursor still reads the recorded 0–360 value. In a comparison, lap B is turned to start within half a turn of lap A.

### Saving on a Mac or PC

- The Mac save dialog suggests the day's name with `.fetproject` once, not twice. On every device, a day named with `.fetproject` at the end no longer gets it twice.
- **Save as…** and **Export for Overlays…** ask **Replace <file>?** before replacing a file the save dialog did not warn about (on Windows and Linux). Cancel saves nothing.

### Install

Android: the `.apk` below. macOS 12 or later: the `-macos.zip` below; it is not notarized; the install page says how to open it the first time. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
