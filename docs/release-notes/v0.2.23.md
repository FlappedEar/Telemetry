## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.23 (pre-release)

The **Progression** card has a new **By lap** view that shows how lap times move through each session and from one session to the next. It installs over 0.2.22 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Progression: By lap

- A chart of lap time by lap number with one line per session, each with its own marker and its name at the end of its line, and a table with a row per session and a column per lap number, so a column is the same point of every session. Each cell shows the lap time and how long after the start of the session's first timed lap it began, such as *+2:31*. Tap a lap in the table to open it.
- Out and in laps are not shown. A lap left out of the ranking reads *not ranked*, breaks its session's line, and is listed below the table with the reason, as in the lap list.
- For each session: the modelled air temperature when its weather is kept; the **first lap in the session's middle half or quicker** and after how many laps (it takes at least three ranked laps), naming a later lap that was quicker by more than the session's spread; and the typical difference **against the session before at the same laps** (at least three laps ranked in both).
- These are observations, not causes: tyres warming up, the track rubbering in or drying, the weather and you learning the track all change together, and the card cannot tell them apart.

### Already in 0.2.22: RaceChrono recordings get segments

- 0.2.22 also included this fix, which its notes left out. Following a lap along the track no longer loses its place on recordings whose track points are unevenly spaced, such as 25 Hz RaceChrono `.rcz` files. Such a day used to get no automatic segments and so no theoretical best; the real day it was found on now gets the same 14 segments as its VBO files. VBO days are unchanged.
