## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.21 (pre-release)

The **Next session** card on **Coach** now has a map with the day's corners numbered, and **Lap to lap in each corner** shows how far apart your lines are where each corner starts, at its apex and where it ends. It installs over 0.2.20 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### Corners numbered on the coach's map

- Above the items of the **Next session** card, a map of the best lap's trace numbers every corner of the day the way the coach names it: *10* on the map is *Corner 10* on the card, *3–4* is *Corners 3–4*, and a corner you renamed shows its name.
- The corner of the selected item is highlighted in purple, with its number larger and on top; the other corners the coach names are drawn in white. The main focus is selected to start with; tap another item to show its corner, and a purple bar beside the item marks the one shown. The map follows the map background you chose for all maps. It is not in the shared report.
- The map under **Why?** now numbers every corner too, and corner names in the coach's items now follow the app's language.

### Line spread at entry, apex and exit

- On the theoretical best card, **Lap to lap in each corner**, the **Line** row is now **Line spread**: how far apart the laps' lines are where the corner starts (*entry*), at its apex (or its middle, for a corner with several) and where it ends (*exit*), next to the recording's GPS accuracy, for example *Line spread: entry 2.3 m, apex 1.8 m, exit 1.1 m · GPS accuracy about 0.15 m*. Before, it showed the apex only.
- A part with fewer than three laps is left out. When the recording states its GPS accuracy, the parts whose spread is no larger than it are named as *not distinguishable from GPS error*. The spread shows where your line changes from lap to lap, not which line is faster.

### Lap detection

- A GPS step is now judged as a gap the same way when finding laps and when checking them, so a lap can no longer be found under one rule and judged under another. Laps on the recordings tested are unchanged.

### Already in 0.2.20: recordings across midnight

- 0.2.20 also included this fix, which its notes left out. Before, a VBO recording whose time of day went backward counted as passing midnight only from after 23:00 to before 01:00, so a recording that lost signal from 22:50 to 01:10 dropped every later row. Now a time that goes backward counts as passing midnight when, read that way, the clock moved forward by at most 3 hours. If the next row is back on the evening before, the row that looked like midnight is skipped instead. Small backward steps, a daylight-saving change and a clock reset during the day are still skipped. Overlays still uses the 23:00 to 01:00 rule.
