## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.22 (pre-release)

A new **G-G envelope** card on the day page shows how hard each session worked the car in each direction, and where the latest session stays short of the day's best. Automatic segments are no longer made or re-measured from a best lap whose GPS line is off, and the profile shows each track day by day. It installs over 0.2.21 and earlier versions and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days. From 0.2.6 on, **Settings › Check for updates** finds this release.

### G-G envelope

- For each of eight directions (accelerating, braking, turning left or right, and braking or accelerating while turning), the card keeps the 95th percentile of the combined G pointing that way, so a single spike does not set it. Only the ranked laps of the group shown count. Samples below 0.1 g have no clear direction and are left out. Readings beyond 4 g are left out too, and the card says how many each session lost that way.
- Each session is a line in its own colour, earlier sessions in blue and green and later ones in yellow; the latest session is the thickest. The **Day's best** in each direction is a dashed purple line.
- The table lists each direction with the day's best and the session it came from, next to the latest session. A latest-session value at least 0.1 g below the day's best is shown in red and bold as **unused envelope**, and the line under the table names each one. A direction with fewer than 25 samples shows —.
- The card names the G channels and whether they were measured or calculated from GPS by the logger. The values are observed accelerations, not a share of the grip available: traffic, a wet track or a different line can lower them as well as the driver.

### Automatic segments only from a lap on the right line

- Before the best lap's corners and straights become the day's segments, its GPS line is compared with up to 8 other ranked laps of the circuit. If, somewhere on the lap, more than half of them are over 30 m away (a GPS error, or a lap that left the track), its segments are not used. On a day without segments, none are made, and the **Theoretical best** card says why: exclude the laps whose line is wrong from the ranking. When a later session's best lap is that far off, the segments are not measured again on it, and the day keeps the ones it has. Excluded and unranked laps are not compared, and with fewer than two other ranked laps the line is not checked.

### Day by day on the profile

- **Profile** has a new **Day by day** card. Each track in each car is listed on its own, one group of lines per day, oldest first (the last 10 days when there are more). For each day it shows the best lap, marked *new best here in this car* when it beat every earlier day there in that car, the typical lap, and four figures the skills measure: braking-point spread, minimum speed below your best, off the throttle to braking, and lap time spread. A figure the day did not measure is left out, never shown as zero. The weather kept for the day's sessions is shown too.
- A trend needs at least 3 dated days at the same track in the same car; with fewer, the card says so and still lists the days. From 3 dated days on, **First day to last** gives the best lap, the typical lap and each of the four figures on the first and last day that measured it, once at least 3 days measured it. Undated days come last and are never a new best or part of a trend.
- Wet or dry is not worked out: the weather comes from a weather model for the area, not from the track surface, and the card says so.

### Already in 0.2.21: tracks across the 180° meridian

- 0.2.21 also included this fix, which its notes left out. Distances, maps, timing lines and lap traces are now worked out the short way round across the 180° line of longitude. Tracks anywhere else are unchanged. Overlays does not take the short way round.
