## FlappedEar Telemetry 0.2.0 (pre-release)

A release candidate for the next track day. It installs over 0.1.0 and over Android builds from `main`, and keeps your saved days.

### New since 0.1.0

- **VBO and RaceChrono RCZ of the same drive are combined.** Each session's VBO and RCZ are aligned on their clocks and the RCZ's extra channels are added; you can make either recording the primary one and check the clock alignment yourself.
- **Edit session details and rename the day:** name, notes, conditions and setup per session.
- **The comparison is saved with the day:** the two laps, the range and the channels you chose come back when you reopen it, also in FlappedEar Overlays.
- **Segments:** pick a segment boundary on the map, review the automatic segment proposals, and see how much each corner varies.
- **Polish.** The app follows the device language: English or Polish.
- **New look** for the Day screen (Day, Laps, Compare) and for Compare and Corners: lap A amber, lap B blue.

### More reliable

- When something unexpected goes wrong, the app says so ("Something went wrong. Details are in Diagnostics.") instead of failing silently, and a part that cannot be drawn says "This part could not be shown." instead of an empty grey box. **Diagnostics → Copy errors** copies the details for a problem report.
- A damaged recording is listed as an error and the rest of the import carries on. The importers were tested against thousands of damaged VBO and RCZ files.
- If unsaved changes cannot be kept for crash recovery (for example, a full phone), the app tells you.
- On a Mac, a save that fails partway (for example, a full disk) keeps the file saved before.
- Android: the signing key behind every APK is now kept alive automatically, so future builds keep installing over this one.

### Install

Android: the `.apk` below. macOS 12 or later: the `-macos.zip` below; it is not notarized, so open it the first time with **Open Anyway** (see the install page). Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
