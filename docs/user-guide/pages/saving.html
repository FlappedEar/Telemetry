Saving and opening days

A day is saved as a .fetproject file. It holds your sessions and your choices, and it points to the recording files rather than copying them.

What a saved day keeps

  • The sessions and where their recordings are.
  • Circuit names and directions you set.
  • Laps you excluded, with their reasons.
  • The group of laps shown, once you chose one. Until then each app shows the group it picks itself.
  • The last comparison: laps A and B, the stretch zoomed to and the charts shown (see Opening a comparison). Opening a comparison of a different pair, zooming or changing its charts therefore leaves the day with unsaved changes.
  • The day's name, and each session's name, conditions, setup changes and notes (see Session details).
  • The segments: the automatic ones become the day's approved segments when you save, and your edits are kept.
  • Which recording is each session's primary (see Choosing the primary recording), the other recording kept with it, and how the two were combined: their clock offset and your choice for channels they disagree on (see VBO and RCZ of one session). This is tied to the content of both recordings. When the day opens, its results show from the VBOs at once and each RCZ is read in the background: while both are unchanged, the saved choice is applied as it is. If the file at the RCZ's place now holds other content, the app checks whether it is still the same drive as the VBO (the same check as when importing both): if it is, the two are lined up again (your choices for channels they still both record are kept), the day remembers the new file and has changes until you save; if not, it is a different recording and that session uses its VBO alone. An RCZ that cannot be found does not stop the day from opening either; that session uses its VBO alone until you find it. A day saved or closed while an RCZ is still being lined up keeps that RCZ, and lines it up when it opens again.

Renaming the day

The day is named Day YYYY-MM-DD from its first dated recording. To change it, open ⋮ › Rename day…, type the new name (up to 160 characters) and tap Save. A blank name cannot be saved. The day then has unsaved changes. A saved day shows the new name in its title at once; a new day shows it from its first save, which offers it as the file name. Renaming the day does not rename a file already saved: on a Mac or PC the file name stays yours to choose.

Saving

  • Save (disk icon) writes to the file the day came from. The first time, it asks where to save. It is greyed out when nothing changed. On a Mac or PC, Cmd+S or Ctrl+S does the same.
  • Save as… (under ⋮) always asks.
  • Adding a session to a saved day saves it again on its own. See Adding a session.

The app confirms with Saved as <file>.fetproject. or says why it could not save. When a save fails, for example because the device is full, the app keeps the file saved before whenever it can. A change you make while the save is still running (excluding a lap, say) is not in that file: the app then adds Changes made while saving are not saved yet., the day stays unsaved, and saving again includes it.

Opening a day

On Import a day, tap Open a saved day….

  • Mac and PC: the system open dialog shows FlappedEar day files.
  • Phone: a list of the days saved in the app, newest first, and Another file… to pick a file from elsewhere.

Find recordings in a folder

If a recording was moved, renamed or deleted, the day still opens. A red card lists each session that could not be opened, and those sessions stay in the day when you save it. A session's RCZ that is missing or is another recording is listed too (The RCZ of 1 session could not be used): on the red card when sessions are missing as well, otherwise on a card of its own in a quieter colour, with the same button.

  1. Save the day first if it has changes.
  2. Tap Find recordings in a folder… and choose the folder that holds the recordings now.
  3. The app recognises each recording by its content, so a renamed file is found. A different recording that only has the same name is never used. A session's RCZ is looked for at the same time; an RCZ with the same name but other content is used only when it is the same drive as the session's VBO, and is then lined up again. Otherwise the app says Not used, a different recording: drive.rcz.
  4. Tap Save to keep the new locations.

If the recordings are back where the day says, for example on a drive that was not connected, tap Retry recordings on the same card (the day must be saved first). The app reads them again, checking each one's content as when the day opens, and shows the day again when more of them open, or to line up a session's RCZ again; otherwise it says The recordings are still not where the day says.

If none of a day's recordings can be used, the app says <day> could not be opened, lists them, and offers Find recordings in a folder… there too.

This needs a folder picker, so it works on a Mac or PC. On a phone it may not work.

On a Mac, the app remembers access to the recordings and folders you chose or dropped, so a saved day reopens after a restart without asking again.
