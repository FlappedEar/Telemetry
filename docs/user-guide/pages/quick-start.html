Quick start

From a day of RaceChrono recordings to your best lap and where it loses time.

  1. Export the day from RaceChrono as VBO (and/or RCZ) files, one per session. On a phone you can share them directly to FlappedEar Telemetry; see Sharing from RaceChrono.
  2. Import. On Import a day, tap Choose recordings… and select all of the day's files. On a Mac or PC you can also choose a folder or drop the files on the window.
  3. Check the sessions. Each recording is listed as Session N with its lap count and best lap. Then tap Show the day's results.
  4. Read the day. The best lap of the day leads, on its map. Below it: the theoretical best, where the time goes, time losses, where to look next, consistency and progression. Tap any lap to open it.
  5. Compare. Tap Compare two laps, or Compare with… on a lap, to see two laps at the same place on the track.

When you are done, tap Save (the disk icon) to keep the day as a .fetproject file. See Saving and opening days.

You do not have to mark sessions, laps or segments by hand. Laps come from the start/finish line in the recording, circuits are recognised from the GPS trace, and corners and straights are proposed from your best lap.
