Phones and tablets

The app works the same on a phone, with a layout made for touch.

Importing on a phone

  • Choose recordings… opens the file picker; select all of the day's VBO/RCZ files. There is no folder picker on a phone.
  • On iPhone the picker shows every file; files that are not recordings are listed under Import notes.

Sharing from RaceChrono

  1. In RaceChrono, open the session and export it as VBO (and/or RCZ).
  2. Choose Share, then FlappedEar Telemetry.
  3. The app opens and adds the file to today's day:
    • If a day is open, the recording is added to it as the next session.
    • Otherwise, if you worked on a day with recordings from the same date in the last 24 hours (unsaved, or the newest saved day), that day opens with the new session added.
    • Otherwise the app imports the file as a new day and opens it. If another day has unsaved changes, the import stays on Import a day next to the offer to restore that day, so nothing is replaced. Recordings you share after it are imported together with it, as one day.

A shared recording is never held for a review, so the next session needs no approval. To check what happens to a file first, choose it with Review the files before importing ticked, or with Add and review recordings… on an open day (see Reviewing the files before importing).

This works on Android and iOS, also when the app was closed. When a session is added, the page moves to the Day section and its Next session card: what to try in your next run.

Layout

  • Below 900 points of width (a phone, or a tablet held upright) the day page has a bar at the bottom with Day, Laps and Compare. Wider screens show the day and the laps side by side, with Day and Compare in a rail at the side.
  • The lap page and the comparison stack the map, charts and cards in one scrolling column.
  • One finger scrolls the page, also over a map. Maps zoom and move with two fingers; a double tap zooms in.
  • Tap a chart, or drag sideways across it, to move the cursor. Dragging up or down over a chart scrolls the page.
  • The lap you chose on a card, a table's mode and the Corner Analyzer's segment stay as they were when you scroll away and back.

Saved days on a phone

Days are saved inside the app (no save dialog) and opened from a list. Uninstalling the app deletes them. See Saving and opening days.
