Compare laps

Two laps at the same place on the track. Lap A is amber, lap B is blue, and Δ is A − B: positive when A is behind.

Opening a comparison

  • The Compare tab under the day's name: Compare two laps, or tap a suggestion under Against the best of the day, which puts each session's best lap (A, amber) against the best lap of the day (B, blue), with the gap on the right.
  • Compare two laps above the lap list: choose lap A, then lap B. Suggested: the fastest picks the group's best lap.
  • Compare with… on a lap: that lap is A; choose B.
  • From the analysis (a time loss, a corner, a focus area): the comparison opens zoomed to that segment, with the Corner Analyzer first.
  • Last comparison above the lap list: the two laps compared last, zoomed to the same stretch and with the same charts. The comparison is saved with the day, so it is there when the day opens again, also when it was set up in FlappedEar Overlays. It is not offered when one of the laps has changed or is no longer ranked. Because the comparison is saved with the day, opening a different pair, zooming or changing the charts leaves the day with unsaved changes.

Only ranked laps of the same group can be compared.

Choosing A and B

  • Tap the A or B badge to pick another lap. Under each lap's time is its session's weather, such as 18 °C, overcast, no rain, wind SW 12 km/h, when the app has it.
  • Swap A and B.
  • B: best of Session N (best of A's session) and B: best of the day. If A is that lap already, the next fastest is used.
  • Lap Δ is the difference in lap time.

The comparison map

Both lines are drawn on the same street, satellite or Apple Maps background as the other maps, with a dot for each lap at the cursor. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to move (on a phone or tablet, use two fingers), and use the layers button to change the background. The menu above the map colours a line by:

  • Line: A / B (default): amber and blue.
  • Speed, Δ time (A−B), Lateral G, Longitudinal G, Throttle, Brake (measured), and each recorded temperature.

When a colouring is chosen, A / B next to the menu picks which lap's line is coloured. If a channel was not recorded, the menu says not recorded; nothing is invented.

Channels by track position

The charts put both laps at the same place on the track, in metres from the start/finish line.

  • The first chart is Δ time (A − B): rising means A is losing time there. Each lap is timed from its own start, so where both laps are recorded up to the line, Δ time is 0 at the start/finish line and equals the Lap Δ at the finish.
  • Then speed, throttle and brake when both laps recorded them. Add, remove or swap channels from a chart's name as on the lap page (up to four). A channel that came from an RCZ in any session of the day says from RCZ.
  • Tap a chart or drag sideways across it to move the cursor; both dots move on the map. With a mouse or trackpad, the cursor follows the pointer.
  • A heading chart is one continuous line through north; the value at the cursor is the recorded 0 to 360, and a screen reader says the heading from where it starts to where it ends, or 0 to 360 when the lap turns all the way round (see the lap page).
  • With a screen reader (TalkBack or VoiceOver), each chart says each lap's lowest and highest value in the stretch shown.
  • Zoom in around the cursor to look at one corner; the map highlights the stretch shown.
  • Open lap A here / Open lap B here open that lap at the same place.

Under the charts

  • Corner Analyzer: corner by corner, A against B.
  • G-G, Driving states and Coasting for the whole lap, or for the stretch you zoomed to.

Reference lap

A reference lap comes from outside the day: a friend's or an instructor's recording, or one of your earlier days. Your laps are compared with it as with lap B, but it stays apart from the day: it is not ranked, not part of the theoretical best, the progression or the coach, and it is not saved in the day's file. Your driver profile remembers it for the day instead: open the day again and the reference is back, with the same lap (see Remembered for the day).

  • At the end of the Compare tab, Load a recording opens the same file picker as adding sessions: choose one VBO or RaceChrono RCZ file (if you pick several, only the first is used, and the app says so). From an earlier day lists the other days of your profile; their recordings must still be where the day found them.
  • The reference is timed on today's start/finish line (the line of the day's best lap), with the same lap timing as your own laps, whatever line the recording had. A VBO and an RCZ give longitudes in opposite conventions; the reference is put in today's before it is timed, so an RCZ works against a VBO day and the other way round. Its fastest complete lap is the reference; Choose its lap picks another, and for an earlier day lists the laps of all its sessions. A lap that day excluded is marked Excluded on its day with its reason, and is never taken as the fastest, but you can still choose it; when every lap was excluded, the app says so and Choose its lap offers them, marked.
  • It is named the same way everywhere: Reference: reference.vbo, lap 2, 1:49.898, or for an earlier day its name and session, such as Reference: Day 2026-08-29 · Session 5, lap 2, 1:49.898.
  • A recording from another track is not used, and the app says why: its GPS never comes within 500 m of today's start/finish line. Its laps must also follow the route of today's best lap the same way round: laps driven the other way, or round another layout that crosses the same line, are refused with that reason. When today's lap has no route to check against (too short, or crossing itself), the app says that only the start/finish line was checked. A recording near the line that never completes a lap across it, one without GPS, and a file that cannot be read are refused with their reason too.
  • The reference is timed on the line shown when it was loaded. When today's line changes (you show another group, or the day's best lap moves to another line), it says it was timed on another start/finish line and compares nothing until you tap Load again.
  • Clear reference drops it, and the profile forgets it: it does not come back when you open the day again.

Remembered for the day

  • The reference you chose is kept in your driver profile, for that day only, and comes back when you open the day again. Under the reference it says Remembered for this day in your driver profile. A day that is not kept in your profile (a day saved as a file somewhere else) says not remembered instead: its reference lives while the day is open.
  • For a recording (a friend's or an instructor's file), the app copies the file into your profile and remembers the copy, so it keeps working when the original is moved or deleted. Only the name of the file is shown; the place it was on your computer is never stored. A copy is kept once, however many days use it, and it travels with the profile when you export it and import it on another device. A recording larger than 128 MiB (about 134 MB), or an RCZ or VBO the profile has no room for, cannot be remembered: the profile keeps at most 32 reference recordings (512 MiB together), and the app says so under the reference, with Try again. The reference still works while the day is open.
  • For a lap of an earlier day, the profile remembers that day and the lap. It works while that day is in your profile; if you delete it, the reference says the day is no longer in your profile, and you can clear it or choose another.
  • If the copy of a recording is missing from the profile, the day still opens and the reference says it was not found. Clear it or load the recording again.
  • If the profile cannot be written (the disk is full, say), the app says the reference is not remembered; it stays while the day is open.
  • Deleting a day deletes its reference with it, and the copy of its recording unless another day's reference or recording uses it. The reference is not part of the day: it is not in the day's file and not in its ranking, theoretical best, progression or coach.

Against the reference

  • Compare with today's best puts the day's best lap (A, amber) against the reference (blue). Tap the A badge to pick another of today's laps.
  • Δ is A − reference: positive when A is behind. Lap Δ is the difference in lap time. It is not shown when the reference lap follows less than 97 % of lap A's line, or when lap A was timed on another start/finish line than the reference.
  • Segments against the reference times today's segments on both laps: A, the reference (Ref) and Δ. The segments are your day's approved ones, or the theoretical best's until the day has its own. A segment one lap does not cover says not timed on one of the laps and why, never a zero.
  • Channels by track position: Δ time, then speed, throttle and brake, by position on today's lap, as in a comparison of two laps.
  • Each chart says the unit of each lap. A unit the recording does not declare is the one the speed unit setting assumes for unlabelled speeds (see Speeds show no unit), and says (assumed). When the two laps are in different units (km/h and mph), or neither declares its speed unit and none is assumed (two loggers may record in different units), they are drawn as two charts, each in its own unit, and never subtracted.

Everything on this page is an observed difference between two laps, not an instruction.
