The lap list

The ranked laps of every session, in the order they were driven. On a phone it is the Laps tab under the day's name.

Laps that are not ranked

Out laps, in laps, sections without a start/finish pass, laps you excluded and laps with an issue are hidden, so the list shows only the laps that count. A session with no ranked lap appears only while they are shown. Show N laps not ranked above the list brings them back, in their place between the other laps; Hide laps not ranked hides them again. The app remembers your choice on this device. Hiding changes only the list: the day, its saved file and the recordings keep every lap. When no lap of the day is ranked, the list shows them all.

Reading a row

  • Title: Session 3 · LAP 2, or · OUT, · IN, · UNKNOWN.
  • Colour, as on a timing screen: a purple edge and time mark the best lap of the day; a green edge and time mark the best lap of its session.
  • Notes under the title: Best of the day, Best of Session N, Excluded: <your reason>, Not ranked: <reason>, Not timed (out and in laps) or No start/finish pass.
  • Time on the right. It is grey when the lap is not ranked or not timed. Under the time of every other ranked lap, the gap to the best lap of the day, such as +0.593 s.

Tap a row to open the lap.

Compare two laps

Above the list, Compare two laps asks for Lap A, then the lap to compare it with. The first choice, Suggested: the fastest, picks the best lap of the group (or the next fastest when A is the best). See Compare laps.

Only ranked laps of the same group can be compared.
