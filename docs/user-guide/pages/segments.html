Editing segments

Segments are the corners and straights the analysis uses. The app proposes them from your best lap; you can rename, move, split, merge or remove them.

Opening the editor

On the day page, in the Theoretical best card, tap Edit segments.

What you see

  • The best lap's map with the segment boundaries as dots, beside the list when the window is wider than it is tall and at least 600 points wide (for example a phone held sideways). The selected segment is blue, with its boundaries as white dots; changes you have not applied yet show as orange dots.
  • Automatic segments or Edited segments, the theoretical best and the number of segments.
  • The segments in lap order: short name (C1, S1…), name, type, start and end in metres from the line, length, and fastest time.

Changing a segment

Tap a segment to select it. Then:

  • Name and type (Corner, Straight, Sector).
  • Start and End: nudge by −10, −1, +1 or +10 m. Move the neighbouring segment too keeps the next segment attached (on by default). Then Apply, or Reset.
  • Split here: set the point with the slider.
  • Merge with the next segment.
  • Remove.

Every change times all laps again. Undo and Redo are in the toolbar.

Keeping your segments

Save the day to keep the segments. Your corrections are saved with the day and are never replaced by automatic segments. Restore automatic goes back to the proposed segments after asking.
