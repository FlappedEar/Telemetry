Day results

The day page leads with your best lap and its map, then explains where the time is. On a wide screen the analysis is on the left and the laps on the right; on a phone they are two tabs, Results and Laps.

The toolbar

  • The title is Day results for a day never saved, otherwise the day's name. A • after the name means there are unsaved changes.
  • Save (disk icon) and, under More (⋮), Save as…. See Saving.
  • Add recordings (list icon with a plus): add the next session to this day. See Adding a session.
  • Day report: the whole day on one page. See Day report.

Adding a session

At the track you do not need to import the whole day again after each session.

  1. On the day page, tap Add recordings and choose the new VBO/RCZ file. On a phone you can also share it from RaceChrono; see Sharing from RaceChrono.
  2. Only the new file is read. It becomes the next session (Session 4 after three), and every card, ranking and the best day update.
  3. A message says Session 4 added to the day. and the page moves to the Next session card, which coaches the session recorded last: the one just added, unless it was recorded before the others. A recording already in the day is not added again. The RCZ of a session added as VBO joins that session as its alternative source instead (RCZ added to Session 2., see VBO and RCZ of one session); a VBO for a session added as RCZ is not added. The message says so.
  4. While the bar shows a session being added, the day stays open: Back asks you to wait until the session is in.

Excluded laps, circuit names and your chosen group are kept. A day that was saved is saved again at once with the new session; a day never saved is kept as unsaved work (see Restoring unsaved work).

Best day

The first card shows the best lap of the day: its time, which session and lap, and its trace coloured by speed (purple slow, yellow fast) with the speed range underneath. Tap it to open the lap.

If there is no best lap, the card says why, for example that no session has enough complete GPS laps to identify its circuit.

Next session

Between sessions, the Next session card suggests what to try in the next run. It looks at the session recorded last, or the one added last when that recording has no time (the line under the heading names it, for example Coaching Session 6 against the day's faster laps), and compares its laps with the faster laps of the whole day, corner by corner.

  • Each item is marked Coach suggestion. It names the corner and what to do, for example Corner 4 · Keep more speed through the slow point.
  • Measured says what the laps show, for example Minimum speed: 46.9 km/h on this session's laps, 53.7 km/h on your faster lap.
  • Try (or Keep, for an improvement over your last three laps) says what to work on. It never gives an exact point, and the braking point stays as it is.
  • Why? opens the measured values (session against faster laps), the laps they come from, the corner highlighted on the best lap's map, and a support score from 0 to 0.9. The score says how well the laps back the pattern; it is not a probability.

There are at most three items: at most two changes, each in a different corner, and one thing to keep. Choose one at a time. The suggestions look for an early lift, too much coasting, a low minimum speed and a late throttle return, using the rules of the earlier DrivingCoach app on this app's own laps and corners. They suggest an opportunity, not a promised gain. A change is only suggested when it was seen on at least three laps of the session.

Speeds carry the unit your recordings declare, without a unit when they declare none, and are never converted. They follow the speed unit setting for recordings that declare no unit. If the recordings declare different units, or a speed channel declares a unit other than km/h, the coach's speeds are left out (shown as —) and the card says why.

When there is nothing to suggest, the card says why: for example that a pattern was seen on fewer than three laps, that no lap of the session has a faster lap to compare with, or that throttle and brake are not recorded (lift, coasting and throttle return then cannot be compared). The Where to look next card under it lists observations: places worth a closer look, without telling you what to do.

Compared laps (groups)

Laps are only ranked against laps driven on the same layout, in the same direction, with the same start/finish line. The app forms these groups by itself. Under the best lap a line such as Group 1 · Detected route · Counterclockwise · 15 of 15 laps ranked says which group the page shows.

When the day has more than one group (for example two layouts), a Compared laps menu lets you pick one. The whole page then follows that group.

Best lap of each session

One row per session with its best ranked lap, for example LAP 2 · 5 of 7 laps ranked · typical 1:50.123. Tap a row to open that lap.

Session details

One row per session with its conditions, setup changes and notes, or No conditions, setup changes or notes. Tap a row to open Details of Session N:

  • Name: the session's name, up to 160 characters, for example Warm-up. It cannot be blank. The new name is used everywhere: the lap list (Warm-up · LAP 2), the results, the progression and the day report.
  • Conditions, for example Dry, 18 °C.
  • Setup changes, for example Tyres +0.1 bar.
  • Notes: anything else about the session.

Save keeps the change and the day then has unsaved changes; save the day to write it to the file. Each text can be up to 4096 characters; a text you clear is saved as empty. FlappedEar Overlays reads and edits the same details (see Working with FlappedEar Overlays).

Circuits

One row per session says which circuit and direction it was matched to, and whether that was inferred from GPS or set by you. Tap the pencil to open Circuit of Session N:

  • Layout name: give the circuit a name, for example Jastrząb full circuit.
  • Clockwise / Counterclockwise: set the direction. On a phone the two buttons are stacked.
  • Also for the sessions on the same route: apply it to the other sessions on that route (on by default).
  • Save regroups the day. Use the detected route removes your setting again.

Use this when a session was put in the wrong group, or its direction is unknown.

VBO and RCZ of one session

When a session has both a VBO and an RCZ of the same drive, the app lines up the two recordings' clocks by their speed traces and combines them by itself, with nothing to confirm. The results show from the VBO straight away; meanwhile the Circuits row says Lining up with its RCZ…, and the combined channels appear when that is done (a few seconds per session):

  • A channel only the RCZ recorded is added to the session. The Circuits row then says, for example, Combined with its RCZ: 1 channel added, and wherever that channel is listed or charted it says from RCZ.
  • A channel both recorded stays the VBO's. Channels the two declare with different units (or one without a unit) are never combined.
  • When the two disagree on a channel, the row says so, for example Satellites: the VBO and the RCZ disagree, with three buttons: Keep VBO (the default), Fill gaps (the VBO, and the RCZ where the VBO has no data) and Use RCZ (the RCZ, and the VBO where the RCZ has no data).
  • When the RCZ was lined up but has nothing to add and agrees with the VBO, the row says so quietly, for example Lined up with its RCZ (−0.14 s); nothing to add.
  • If lining up an RCZ you just added fails, the app says RCZ added to Session 2, but it could not be combined; it is kept and tried again when the day opens. and the row says Not combined with its RCZ: lining them up failed.
  • When the clocks cannot be lined up, nothing is combined and the row says why in one line, for example Not combined with its RCZ: their speed traces do not line up clearly.

Laps, lap times and the ranking always come from the VBO alone, as does the theoretical best; the combined channels are used by lap charts, comparisons and the Car and Driver cards. Otherwise a session whose RCZ added nothing and agrees with its VBO looks as before.

When a saved day's RCZ is not where the day says, or the file there is another recording, a card lists it (The RCZ of 1 session could not be used) with Find recordings in a folder…; the session uses its VBO alone until then.

Notes

At the bottom, Notes lists messages from the analysis, each starting with the session it is about. Sessions whose circuit could not be identified are listed under their own heading; their laps are not compared.

Missing recordings

A saved day only points to its recording files. If one was moved or deleted, the day still opens, and a red card at the top lists the sessions that could not be opened. See Find recordings in a folder.
