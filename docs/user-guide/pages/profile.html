Your profile and skills

Profile, in the bar at the bottom or the rail at the side, adds up every day in your library: how much you have driven, your records at each track, and your skills. It appears once the app keeps a library.

All days

Days, sessions, timed laps, distance, time on track (time moving), tracks and cars, over every day in your library. Distance and time come from each session's recording. A day saved before this version has none until you open it again; until then a line says how many sessions the distance and time cover, and with none measured they show —.

Skills

The skills follow DrivingCoach's 12-skill model, grouped as Braking, Corner, Exit and Lap. Each skill the app measures has a level from 1 to 5, where 5 is the best, worked out over your last 3 days that measured it. Beside the level:

  • Confidence, from the ranked laps measured: low below 5, medium from 5 to 14, high from 15.
  • How many ranked laps over how many days it comes from, and whether it is improving, steady or declining against the days before.
  • The measured value the level comes from.

A measured skill with no lap measured yet says Needs more evidence. The other eight skills say Not measured by the app yet. The levels are against fixed bands, not a score in percent, and like the coach they point at an opportunity, never a promised gain.

The same skills appear under Coach, below the Next session card.

Cars and tracks

Cars lists each car you have driven with its days, distance and time on track. Tracks lists each track with its direction and visits, and your records there: the best lap, the best theoretical lap and the best typical lap (the median of a session), each with its date and session. When you have been to a track more than once, a line says how much faster or slower the best lap of your last visit was than the visit before.

Corners that keep costing time

A corner is listed when it was among the three corners that cost the most time (0.1 s or more) on two or more visits to a track. Each shows the time lost on average and on how many visits, and how it stands now: Still costing time when it lost time on one of the last two visits that measured it, Fading when the last two visits since it last lost time did not measure it (no lap was read there), and Not lost on the last two visits when both measured it without losing time. With one visit to a track the list is empty and says so.
