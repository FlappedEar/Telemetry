FlappedEar Telemetry User Guide

FlappedEar Telemetry turns a track day of RaceChrono recordings into lap times, your best lap on the map, and where the time goes. It runs on Android, iPhone and iPad, macOS and Windows.

What it does

  • Import a day. Choose the day's VBO and RCZ files, or share them straight from RaceChrono on a phone. Each recording becomes a session (Session 1, Session 2, …) with its laps.
  • See the best lap first. The day opens on its fastest lap, drawn on the track map and coloured by speed.
  • Find the time. The theoretical best, time losses per corner and straight, and a short list of where to look next.
  • Compare two laps at the same place on the track: Δ time, speed, pedals, the Corner Analyzer, G-G, driving states and coasting.
  • Save the day as a .fetproject file that FlappedEar Overlays opens too.

Telemetry has no video. Onboard video and overlays are in the separate app, FlappedEar Overlays.

Words used in this guide

WordMeaning
SessionOne recording (one outing on track). Sessions are numbered in the order they were recorded.
LAP n, OUT, INA lap between two crossings of the start/finish line, the out lap before the first crossing, the in lap after the last one.
RankedA complete timed lap on the same circuit and direction as the others, so it counts for the best lap and the analysis.
SegmentA corner, straight or sector of the track. The app proposes them from your best lap; you can edit them.
Theoretical bestThe fastest time of each segment, added up across all laps.
Typical, spreadTypical is the median lap; spread is the interquartile range (the width of the middle half of the laps). Both need at least three laps.
Δ (delta)A − B. Positive means lap A is behind.

About this guide

Every screenshot here is taken from the app itself, using a made-up demo day: three sessions on an invented 2 km circuit, created for the guide. No real recording is shown. The pictures are made without network, so the street map behind each trace is left plain grey; in the app you see the streets or satellite imagery of the real track.

The guide describes the app as it is on the main branch. It is updated with every change to what you see on screen; if something here does not match your app, your build is probably older than this page.

Results in the app are observations from your recordings. They say what differed between laps, not why, and they are never driving instructions.
