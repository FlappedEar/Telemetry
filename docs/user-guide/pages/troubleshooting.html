Troubleshooting

A session has no laps

The message under the session on Import a day says why:

MessageWhat to do
No laps: the recording has no start/finish line.In RaceChrono, set the track's start/finish line, then export again.
No laps: the recording has more than one start/finish line.Keep one start/finish line on the track in RaceChrono and export again.
No laps: the start/finish line is not valid.Check the line's position in RaceChrono.
No laps: the recording has no usable GPS.The recording has no GPS positions the app can use, so no start/finish line can time laps. This is shown before any message about the line. Check that the logger had a GPS fix during the session. On the Day screen the note reads No GPS positions in this recording; laps cannot be timed.
No complete laps: the start/finish line was not crossed often enough.The session needs at least two crossings for a lap.

A lap is not in the ranking

Open the lap list and read the note under the lap. See Why a lap is not ranked. A session on the wrong circuit or direction can be fixed in Circuits.

Nothing was imported

  • No VBO or RCZ recordings were found: check the folder; tick Include subfolders if the files are in subfolders.
  • import at most 64 at a time: choose fewer files or a smaller folder.
  • Some files were not imported: a recording can be at most 128 MB, and one import at most 256 MB. or Choose at most 64 recordings at a time. (Android): choose fewer or smaller files, or share the day's sessions in more than one go.
  • Finish the current import first: wait for the running import or tap Cancel.
  • Another day is being opened. Try again after it.: a recording shared from RaceChrono opened today's day while you were choosing one. Close it, then open the day again.

A saved day shows sessions that could not be opened

The recordings were moved, renamed or changed. Use Find recordings in a folder, or Retry recordings when they are back where they were.

The map is empty or grey

  • Street, satellite and Apple Maps backgrounds need a network connection the first time a track is shown. The trace is still drawn without them.
  • No GPS recorded for this section. means the lap has no positions.

A chart says "No data in this range" or "Not available"

The channel was not recorded in that part of the lap, or not at all. The app never fills a gap or substitutes another channel.

Speeds show no unit

Speeds carry the unit the recording declares (RaceChrono writes km/h into its files). When a recording does not say, open Settings (the gear in the toolbar; on a phone's day page, in the ⋮ menu) and choose the Unit for unlabelled speeds: km/h or mph. This only names the unit of recordings that declare none; a declared unit is never replaced, and no value is converted. A day whose recordings use different units shows speeds without a unit.

Android will not update the app

See the note in Install. Do not uninstall before your days are saved elsewhere.

"Something went wrong" or "This part could not be shown"

The app met an error it did not expect. Something went wrong. Details are in Diagnostics. appears at the bottom of the screen (once for several errors close together); a part of a page that could not be drawn shows This part could not be shown. instead. The rest of the app keeps working, and your day is not changed by the error. A file that could not be read is listed as an error in the import, and the other files are still imported.

Open Diagnostics from the More menu of Import a day or of the day page. Under Errors it lists what went wrong since the app started, newest first, and an error that repeats once with how often it happened; Copy errors copies them with their technical details for a problem report. The list is kept only while the app runs. It also lists when unsaved changes could not be kept for recovery, for example because the device is full: save the day then.

Reporting a problem

Note what you did, what you expected and what happened, with a screenshot, and which build you use (the release version, or the commit in the CI artifact's name). If Diagnostics lists errors, add them with Copy errors.
